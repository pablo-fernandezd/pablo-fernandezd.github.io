ClearPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Access

ClearPhone accesses photos and videos stored on your device solely to display them for review. We read file metadata (name, date, size) and thumbnails. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files to any server.

2. Data Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device using a local database:

  • IDs of media files you have reviewed (to avoid showing them again)
  • File hashes for duplicate detection (computed and stored locally)
  • Cleanup statistics (number of files deleted, space freed)
  • App preferences (undo buffer size, onboarding completion)

This data never leaves your device.

3. Permissions

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: To display your photos and videos for review.
  • READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required on older Android versions for media access.
  • INTERNET: Used exclusively for displaying ads (AdMob) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: To show progress during background duplicate scanning.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS: To display app cache and storage sizes.

4. Third-Party Services

ClearPhone uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Displays banner and interstitial ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Collects crash reports to help us fix bugs. This includes device model, OS version, and stack traces. No personal data or media content is included.

5. Data Deletion

When you delete media through ClearPhone, the files are permanently removed from your device via the Android system API. You can clear all app data from Android Settings to reset all local preferences and review history.

6. Children's Privacy

ClearPhone is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
